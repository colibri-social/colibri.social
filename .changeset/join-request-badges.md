Show the number of pending join requests on the Space name, the Manage Space entry and the Members entry in Space settings, for people who can review them. The count follows requests accepted or denied on other devices and by other admins. The Join modal closes and opens the Space once a pending request is accepted. Voice channel chat no longer lights the Space unread dot, and mentions there still count toward the mention badge.
