Links to channels, threads and messages in your other Spaces show as pills with the Space icon, and links you can't open show "No access". Message pills end in a speech bubble icon instead of the word "Message", and emoji pack pills use the emoji icon. Pill icons are slightly smaller and centered. moji.blue pack links show the emoji pack card instead of the moji.blue preview. Pressing Escape in the composer removes all attachments and cancels the reply. A message made only of custom emoji shows big placeholders while they load. The video fullscreen button goes straight to fullscreen instead of the media viewer.
