Quick reactions now follow the emoji you actually use. The client keeps a local tally of every emoji you react with, ranked by how often you use it and broken by which you reached for most recently, and both reaction shortcuts read from that tally. Defaults fill any gaps, so the rows look complete before you have reacted to anything.
