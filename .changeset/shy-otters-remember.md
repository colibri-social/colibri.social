Mention and member search now ignore accents, so typing `@Jose` finds José and `@Jorgen` finds Jørgen. Folding also covers letters that do not simply decompose, including ø, ł, đ, ß and æ, and it applies everywhere members and roles are searched by name: the composer's `@` and `#` pickers, the channel allow-list editor, and the role list in community settings.
