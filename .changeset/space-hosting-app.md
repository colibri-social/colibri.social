Create a Space on an account you already own: pick "Use an account I own" under Hosting, sign in as that account, and Colibri sets the Space up once you're back. Space owners find a new Hosting page in the danger zone of Space settings that shows where the Space's account lives, and asks them to sign in again when Colibri loses access. A Space that needs a sign-in shows a warning on its icon and next to its name for the owner. Moving a Space to another server is built but stays hidden until servers can carry Spaces over
