Colibri is exiting allowlist-gated early access. Anyone with an AT Protocol account can sign in now, and the join-the-waitlist prompts and download page are gone/back accordingly (the allowlist itself isn't removed, just switched off, so it can be re-enabled later if needed). To keep public channels safe, the first time you try to chat on a device you'll be asked to acknowledge a short guidelines notice (channels are public, don't share sensitive info) before your message goes out.
