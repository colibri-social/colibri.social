Emoji shortcode suggestions now match keywords, so typing ":salute" suggests the saluting face even though its shortcode is "saluting_face". Results are ranked by match quality instead of alphabetically, up to ten suggestions are shown, each emoji appears only once, and the emoji picker search also matches keywords.
