A **Space** is where people gather into categories and channels to talk. In
Colibri, a Space is itself an AT Protocol identity with its own DID.

A Space is a set of gated [**ATproto spaces**](/docs/developers/architecture/spaces)
whose authority is the Space's DID. Each member's messages live in that member's
own repository, and a reader reaches them through the ATproto space that
holds them.

:::note[Space and community]
The protocol and the lexicons call a Colibri Space a **community**, as in
`social.colibri.beta.community.*`. These docs write "Space" with a capital
letter for the product concept, "ATproto space" or lowercase "space" for
the protocol primitive, and keep `community` wherever an identifier uses it.
:::

## Space types

Colibri declares eight space types. Seven of them have the Space's DID as
their authority.

| Space type | Key | Holds | Who can read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `social.colibri.beta.community.profile` | `self` | the community record | anyone |
| `social.colibri.beta.community.configuration` | `self` | settings, categories, bridge registrations, labels | members |
| `social.colibri.beta.community.members` | `self` | members, roles, labels | members |
| `social.colibri.beta.community.moderation` | `self` | the moderation log | holders of `moderation.viewLog` |
| `social.colibri.beta.channel.text` | `tid` | channel, messages, reactions, labels | per channel |
| `social.colibri.beta.channel.voice` | `tid` | the channel record, and the messages of the voice channel's chat | per channel |
| `social.colibri.beta.channel.thread` | `tid` | thread, thread follows, messages, reactions, labels | per thread |

The first four are created once, when the Space is created. The last three are
space *types*, and a Space has one ATproto space of that type per channel
or thread.

The eighth type, `social.colibri.beta.actor.preferences`, belongs to a user.
See [the personal space](#the-personal-space).

### A channel is its space

The ATproto space **is** the channel. Its key is the channel's identity,
and the channel's own configuration lives at record key `self` inside it.

Creating a channel creates an ATproto space, and deleting one deletes it.

### A thread is its space

A thread works the same way. Its configuration is the
`social.colibri.beta.thread` record at key `self` in its own
`social.colibri.beta.channel.thread` space. That record names the text channel
the thread belongs to, and only the Space writes it.

A thread is readable by everyone who can read its parent channel, unless its
record lists `visibleToRoles` or `visibleToMembers`.

### The personal space

Each user also has one ATproto space of their own,
`social.colibri.beta.actor.preferences`, whose authority is the user's own DID.
It holds their mutes, blocks, settings, read cursors and emoji follows.

Its policy is a member list with no members. The space host authorizes the
owner before it consults the policy, so the owner is the only reader. The owner
grants the AppView access with
`social.colibri.beta.actor.grantSpaceAccess`.

## Where records live

The repository a record is written to identifies who is accountable for it.

### Written by the Space

The AppView holds the Space's credentials and writes these on its behalf.
They are the Space's structure and its authoritative state. The AppView indexes
one of these records only when the Space's own DID is its author.

| Record | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `social.colibri.beta.community` | The Space's name, description, picture and banner, and the `managingApp` that runs it. At key `self` in the profile space. |
| `social.colibri.beta.community.settings` | Category order, join approval, link embeds, name colours (`overrideUserNameColors`), the owner badge (`hideOwnerBadge`), the custom emoji policy (`customEmoji`), and the labelers this Space honours. |
| `social.colibri.beta.category` | A category, holding an ordered list of channel keys and its permission overrides. |
| `social.colibri.beta.role` | A role and its permissions. |
| `social.colibri.beta.member` | A member: their DID, their roles, their nickname and when they joined. Keyed by the subject's DID. |
| `social.colibri.beta.channel` | A channel's configuration and permission overrides, at key `self` in the channel's own space. |
| `social.colibri.beta.thread` | A thread's configuration, at key `self` in the thread's own space. |
| `social.colibri.beta.bridge.registration` | A bridge the Space has paired with, and the channels it relays. In the configuration space. |
| `social.colibri.beta.moderation` | The append-only log of bans, unbans and kicks. |

`social.colibri.beta.member` is keyed by the subject's DID, so looking someone up
is a direct fetch, and a Space cannot hold two member records for the same
person.

A bridge writes messages, reactions and threads through the AppView as the
Space. Those records live in the Space's repository and carry a `bridged`
attribution. See
[Building a Bridge](/docs/developers/bridges/building-a-bridge).

### Written by each member

A member writes these to their own repository, and readers reach them through
the ATproto space.

| Record | Space | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `social.colibri.beta.message` | channel or thread | A message. |
| `social.colibri.beta.reaction` | channel or thread | A reaction, targeting a message by author and key. |
| `social.colibri.beta.label` | channel or thread | A label on some content. The AppView honours it when its author is one of the Space's labelers. |
| `social.colibri.beta.thread.follow` | thread | Marks its author as following the thread. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.mute` | preferences | One muted user, Space, channel or thread. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.block` | preferences | One blocked user. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.settings` | preferences | Notification level, Space order, GIF favourites, activity sharing. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.clientPreferences` | preferences | Client settings that follow the user across devices. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.composerState` | preferences | Emoji usage, skin tone and recent GIFs. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.spaceNotification` | preferences | A notification level for one Space. The record key is the Space's DID. |
| `social.colibri.beta.actor.spaceModeration` | preferences | Moderation view options for one Space. The record key is the Space's DID. |
| `social.colibri.beta.channel.read` | preferences | Read cursors, one record per Space. |
| `social.colibri.beta.emoji.follow` | preferences | A followed custom emoji pack or emoji. |

`social.colibri.beta.actor.profile` is the one public record. It lives in the
user's ordinary repository, outside any ATproto space.

Your messages are stored in your own repository. You own them, they travel with
your atproto identity, and deleting one is an ordinary record deletion. A reader
needs a credential for the channel's space to see them.

A member whose PDS cannot hold space repositories has them hosted by the
AppView. See [Hosting](/docs/developers/architecture/hosting).

## Joining

Joining is the `social.colibri.beta.community.join` procedure, documented in
the [AppView specification](/docs/developers/specification/appview). The AppView
refuses a banned caller, checks the invitation when one is given, and then either
writes the `member` record or stores a pending application for a Space that
requires approval. A new member receives every role marked `default`.

From then on, the AppView answers `read: true` when the Space's PDS asks whether
you may read that Space's ATproto spaces.

## What creating a Space does

`social.colibri.beta.community.create` runs these steps.

<Steps>

1. Creates an account on the AppView's configured PDS and stores its password,
   encrypted.
2. Creates the profile, configuration, members and moderation spaces.
3. Writes the community record, an Owner role holding every permission, a
   Members role marked `default`, and a member record for the creator holding
   both roles.
4. Seeds a layout: a `general` text channel and a `General` voice
   channel, each in its own space, grouped into a "Text channels" and a "Voice
   channels" category, plus the settings record.

</Steps>

A channel's space is created before the record that describes it, so a failure
part way through leaves an empty space behind.

A Space can also live on an account that already exists on another PDS. See
[Hosting](/docs/developers/architecture/hosting#bring-your-own-hosting).
