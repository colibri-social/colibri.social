A member's space repositories live on that member's PDS or on the AppView. A
Space's account lives on the AppView's own PDS or on a PDS its owner chose.
This page covers both.

## The spaces polyfill

A member's messages, reactions and preferences live in space repositories on
their own PDS. That requires a PDS that serves the `com.atproto.space` methods.
For a member whose PDS does not, the AppView hosts those repositories itself.
This is the **spaces polyfill**.

The polyfill covers a member's own repositories. A Space's account always needs
a PDS that serves spaces. See
[when a spaces PDS is required](#when-a-spaces-pds-is-required).

### Detecting support

The AppView probes a member's PDS to learn whether it serves spaces. It first
reads the method list from `community.lexicon.service.describe` and looks for
`com.atproto.space.getDelegationToken`. When the PDS offers no method list, the
AppView compares the PDS's answer to that method with its answer to a method
that does not exist.

The result is cached per PDS: 24 hours for a PDS that serves spaces, and one
hour for one that does not.

### Host modes

Every member has a host mode, and each of their space repositories has one too.
A repository without its own mode follows the member's, and a member the
AppView has never recorded is `native`.

| Mode | Where the repository lives | Who accepts writes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `native` | On the member's PDS. The AppView syncs from it. | The PDS |
| `polyfill` | On the AppView | The AppView |
| `migrating` | On the AppView, while the client copies it to the PDS | The AppView |

A repository moves between modes along these transitions: `polyfill` to
`migrating`, `migrating` to `native`, `migrating` back to `polyfill`, and
`native` to `polyfill`.

A member enters `polyfill` when one of these happens:

- `social.colibri.beta.polyfill.getStatus` finds that their PDS does not serve
  spaces.
- Their first `social.colibri.beta.polyfill.applyWrites` arrives and the probe
  says their PDS does not serve spaces.
- A sync pull of one of their repositories fails in a way that shows the PDS
  does not serve spaces, and a fresh probe confirms it.

### Reading and writing

`social.colibri.beta.polyfill.getStatus` reports the caller's mode and whether
their PDS serves spaces. The client reads it and routes space reads and writes
to the PDS or to the AppView.

`social.colibri.beta.polyfill.applyWrites` creates, updates and deletes records
in the caller's own repository in one space. The AppView authorizes the write
with the same decision it gives the Space's PDS through `checkAccess`, validates
each record against its lexicon, and applies the batch as one revision in one
transaction. It then indexes the records the same way it indexes a synced write.

A record keeps the URI it will have on the member's PDS after a migration.

The polyfill accepts these collections:

| Space | Collections |
| --- | --- |
| Text channel, voice channel, thread | `social.colibri.beta.message`, `social.colibri.beta.reaction`, `social.colibri.beta.label` |
| Thread | `social.colibri.beta.thread.follow` |
| The caller's preferences space | `actor.mute`, `actor.block`, `actor.settings`, `actor.clientPreferences`, `actor.composerState`, `actor.spaceNotification`, `actor.spaceModeration`, `channel.read`, `emoji.follow` |

`social.colibri.beta.polyfill.getRecord` and `listRecords` read the caller's own
hosted repository. A call for a repository the AppView does not host fails with
`RepoNotHosted`.

For each hosted repository, the AppView keeps a revision and a set hash over
its records, the same hash a PDS signs in a commit. The
[sync engine](/docs/developers/architecture/sync) skips hosted repositories.

### Blobs

`social.colibri.beta.polyfill.uploadBlob` stores an attachment for a member the
AppView hosts. Blobs go to an S3-compatible bucket, configured with the
`POLYFILL_S3_*` variables:

| Variable | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `POLYFILL_S3_BUCKET` | The bucket name. Leave it unset to turn uploads off. |
| `POLYFILL_S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `POLYFILL_S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY` | The bucket credentials. Set the bucket name and both credentials together. |
| `POLYFILL_S3_ENDPOINT` | The endpoint of an S3-compatible service |
| `POLYFILL_S3_REGION` | The region, `us-east-1` by default |
| `POLYFILL_S3_FORCE_PATH_STYLE` | Set to `true` for services that need the bucket in the path (e.g. SeaweedFS, MinIO) |

The AppView's Compose files ship a SeaweedFS bucket server in the `blobs`
profile.

A blob can be up to 75 MB. An upload without a configured bucket fails with
`BlobStorageUnavailable`. The AppView deletes a blob that no record references
within 24 hours of its upload.

### Migrating to a PDS

A member's repositories move to their PDS once it serves spaces. The client
drives the move, and the AppView stays authoritative for each repository until
its copy verifies.

<Steps>

1. The client reads `getStatus`. A mode of `polyfill` with
   `pdsSupportsSpaces: true` starts a migration.

2. The client calls `social.colibri.beta.polyfill.beginMigration`. The AppView
   probes the PDS and fails with `SpacesUnsupported` when it does not serve
   spaces. Otherwise the member and each of their hosted repositories enter
   `migrating`, and the call returns the spaces that still have to move. Calling
   it again resumes an interrupted migration.

3. For each space, the client pages through
   `social.colibri.beta.polyfill.listForMigration`, fetches every referenced blob
   with `social.colibri.beta.polyfill.getBlob`, and writes the blobs and records
   to its PDS.

4. The client calls `social.colibri.beta.polyfill.completeMigration` for the
   space. The AppView fetches and verifies the repository from the PDS and
   compares its set hash with its own.

5. On a match, the repository becomes `native` and the AppView seeds its sync
   cursor from the fetched revision. On a mismatch, the repository returns to
   `polyfill` and the response lists the differing records in `missing`. When
   the PDS copy cannot be read, the repository returns to `polyfill` and the call
   fails with `VerificationFailed`.

</Steps>

The AppView accepts writes to a `migrating` repository throughout. The member
becomes `native` once every one of their repositories is `native`, and
`getStatus` reports progress as `spacesTotal` and `spacesDone` until then.

## Bring your own hosting

`social.colibri.beta.community.create` creates a new account for the Space on
the PDS at the AppView's `PDS_URL`. That needs `PDS_ADMIN_PASSWORD`, and the
AppView keeps the account's generated password.

A Space can live on an account that already exists on another PDS. The account
keeps its DID, its handle and its PDS, and the caller becomes the Space's
owner. The AppView creates the four ATproto spaces of a Space on that PDS
and seeds the same starter layout `create` does.

Wherever its account lives, a Space is served by the AppView named in the
`managingApp` field of its `social.colibri.beta.community` record. See
[One AppView per Space](/docs/developers/architecture/spaces#one-appview-per-space).

### Signing in as the host account

`social.colibri.beta.community.authorizeHost` starts an OAuth sign-in as the
host account.

<Steps>

1. The client calls `authorizeHost` with the account's handle or DID and an
   `intent` of `adopt` or `reconnect`. The AppView answers with an
   `authorizeUrl` and a `request` identifier.

2. The user opens the URL, signs in as that account on its own PDS, and allows
   the AppView to act for it.

3. The client polls `social.colibri.beta.community.getHostAuthorization` with
   the `request`. Its `status` is `pending`, then `done` or `failed`. A failure
   carries one of `cancelled`, `wrongAccount`, `spacesUnsupported`,
   `alreadyHosts` or `failed`.

</Steps>

With `adopt`, the AppView turns the account into a new Space. With `reconnect`,
an administrator of an existing Space restores the AppView's access after its
sign-in lapsed or the account moved to another PDS.

Host sign-in needs `HOST_OAUTH_PRIVATE_KEY`, an ES256 key the AppView signs its
client assertions with. Without it, `authorizeHost` fails with
`HostSignInUnavailable`. The AppView serves its OAuth client metadata at
`/oauth/host/client-metadata.json`. `HOST_OAUTH_KEY_ID` names the key, and
`HOST_OAUTH_RETURN_URL` sets where the browser lands after the sign-in.

### Adopting with a password

`social.colibri.beta.community.adopt` does the same with the account's
identifier and password. The credentials must authenticate as the exact DID
given. An app password does not work, because minting a delegation token
requires full account access.

`social.colibri.beta.community.registerCredentials` hands the AppView an
identifier and password for a Space's account. The AppView checks them against
the account's PDS before it stores them, and only an administrator of the Space
can replace credentials the AppView already holds.

The AppView stores passwords and OAuth sessions encrypted with AES-256-GCM
under `CREDENTIAL_ENCRYPTION_KEY`.

### Checking where a Space lives

`social.colibri.beta.community.getHosting` describes a Space's account to its
administrators. `kind` is `colibri` when the account lives on the AppView's PDS
and the AppView holds its password, and `own` when the AppView acts through a
sign-in. `needsSignIn` is `true` when the AppView lost access. New writes fail
until an administrator reconnects the account.

The AppView watches identity events for the accounts it holds credentials for.
When an account's PDS changes, it marks the Space as needing a new sign-in.

### Handing over a hosted account

`social.colibri.beta.community.handOverHost` lets an administrator move an
account that lives on the AppView's PDS to another PDS.

The AppView sets the given email address on the account, so the codes the PDS
sends for the move reach the administrator, and returns the account's handle
and a new password once. The password works for 60 minutes. When the account
has moved by then, the Space is marked as needing a sign-in on its new PDS. When
it has not, the AppView replaces the password.

The call fails with `HandOverUnavailable` when the account does not live on the
AppView's PDS, when it has already moved, or when the AppView has no
`PDS_ADMIN_PASSWORD`.

## When a spaces PDS is required

| Account | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| A member | Any PDS. The polyfill hosts the member's space repositories when the PDS does not serve spaces. |
| A Space created with `create` | The PDS at `PDS_URL` must serve spaces. |
| A Space adopted with `authorizeHost` or `adopt` | The account's PDS must implement the `com.atproto.space` policy methods. Adoption fails with `SpacesUnsupported` when it does not. |
