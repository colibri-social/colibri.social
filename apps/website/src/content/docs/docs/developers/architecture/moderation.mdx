A message lives in its author's own repository, and only its author can delete
it.

A Space controls **who may read its ATproto spaces** and **what it says
about the content in them**.

## Operations are labels

Hiding a message, moving it, marking it a spoiler and suppressing its embeds are
all [`social.colibri.beta.label`](/docs/developers/specification/lexicons) records,
written into the same ATproto space as the content they describe.

A label shares the access boundary of the record it labels, so a label on a
message in a private channel is readable only by people who can read that
channel.

Retracting a label writes a **negating label** and leaves the original in
place, so a reader moving forward through the log sees that the label was
withdrawn.

| Label | What the AppView does |
| --- | --- |
| `hidden` | Withholds the record |
| `moved` | Withholds the record from this space and serves it in the space named by the label's `destination` |
| `spoiler` | Passes the label through for the client to honour |
| `embeds-suppressed` | Passes the label through for the client to honour |

### `hidden` is withheld

A message carrying a `hidden` label from a labeler the Space honours is
**not served**. It is absent from `listMessages`, from `listReactions`,
from notifications, from unread counts and from the events socket.

A moderator and the author still receive it:

- A moderator holding `label.apply` in that channel receives it with its label
  attached, since reviewing and retracting a hide requires seeing what was
  hidden.
- The author receives their own, with its label attached. The record is in their
  repository and they can read it from there at any time.

A reply whose parent is withheld gets a `deletedMessageView` stand-in in place
of the parent, identical to the one an author's own deletion produces.

Hiding stops **this AppView** from serving the record. The message stays in its
author's repository, and anyone holding a credential for the space can read it
directly from the repo host, or run an AppView that ignores the label. Describe
such a message as hidden in a client's UI.

### `moved` is served elsewhere

`social.colibri.beta.thread.moveMessages` moves messages into another thread or
channel. It writes one `moved` label per message into the source space, each
naming the `destination` space, and labels written in one move share a `batch`
value.

The AppView withholds a moved message from the space it was written in and
serves it in the destination at its own record key, so it reads among the
destination's messages by send time.

### `spoiler` and `embeds-suppressed` are passed through

These are display instructions. The AppView forwards them on
`messageView.labels` and the client honours them.

## A Space can be moderated by more than itself

A labeler is an admitted member of the space writing labels into its own
repository. The protocol treats it like any other member.

`social.colibri.beta.community.settings` carries a `labelers` list, which is the set
of DIDs whose labels this Space honours in addition to its own. The AppView
ignores labels from anyone else when it serves a read.

## Banning is a credential decision

Banning someone deletes their `social.colibri.beta.member` record and stops the
Space issuing them credentials. The next time their client asks the Space's PDS
to read one of its ATproto spaces, the PDS asks the AppView, and the
AppView answers `read: false`.

The refusal happens at the protocol layer, before any data moves.

Their existing messages stay in their own repository, where they can be
labelled and filtered.

`social.colibri.beta.moderation` records that the ban happened. It is the audit
log, and it holds `ban`, `unban` and `kick` entries.

Whether someone is currently banned is decided by the most recent `ban` or
`unban` entry for them, ordered by record key. The key is a TID and therefore
monotonic. `createdAt` is author supplied, and two entries can share a
millisecond.

## Roles and permissions

A role carries a list of permissions and a `position`. A higher position
outranks a lower one. Every member holds the roles marked `default`, which
includes the Members role created with the Space.

A channel record and a category record each carry `permissionOverrides`. One
override names either a role or a member, and lists the permissions it allows
and the permissions it denies there. A deny in an override wins over an allow in
the same override.

The AppView resolves a permission in a channel in this order:

<Steps>

1. The Space's own DID and every holder of the protected Owner role have every
   permission. A banned account has none.
2. A caller who is not a member, or who lacks `channel.view` in the channel,
   has no permission there.
3. The channel's override for the member.
4. The channel's overrides for the member's roles, from the highest position
   down, with default roles last.
5. The category's overrides, in the same order as steps 3 and 4.
6. The base: the permission lists of the member's roles.

</Steps>

Outside a channel, only steps 1 and 6 apply.

An override can carry these permissions: `channel.view`, `channel.update`,
`channel.delete`, `message.send`, `message.react`, `thread.create`,
`thread.manage`, `thread.move`, `mention.roles`, `label.apply`, `voice.connect`,
`voice.speak`, `voice.listen`, `voice.camera`, `voice.screenshare`, `voice.chat`
and `voice.moderate`.

Every action taken *against another person* is additionally guarded by
hierarchy: you must strictly outrank them. The Space itself outranks everyone,
and someone with no roles outranks nobody. Two members whose highest roles share
a position cannot ban each other.

:::note[Deprecated fields]
The lexicons mark `ownerOnly`, `allowedRoles`, `allowedMembers`,
`visibleToRoles` and `visibleToMembers` on channel and category records, and
`channelOverrides` on role records, as deprecated. The AppView reads them only
for a channel or category record that has no `permissionOverrides`.
:::

## Reading and posting are separate

Reading a channel is the `channel.view` permission, and posting is
`message.send`. In a voice channel, reading the text chat is `voice.chat`, and
posting there needs both `voice.chat` and `message.send`. Joining the call is
`voice.connect`, which posting in the chat doesn't need.

A read-only announcement channel denies `message.send` to the Members role and
allows it to the roles that post there, while `channel.view` stays with
everyone. A voice channel whose chat is read-only denies `message.send` the same
way.

Reacting is `message.react`, and it resolves on its own. A member can hold it in
a channel that denies them `message.send`, and the other way around. Reacting in
a voice channel's chat also needs `voice.chat`. Every member holds
`message.react` by default, like `channel.view`, `message.send` and
`voice.connect`.

A channel is private when it, or its category, denies `channel.view` to the
default role. The AppView refuses space credentials for it to every member
whose `channel.view` resolves to a deny.

A thread follows its parent channel. A thread whose record lists
`visibleToRoles` or `visibleToMembers` is readable only by those roles and
members, by holders of the Owner role, and by the Space itself. Writing in a
thread needs the right to post in the parent channel, and a locked thread also
needs `thread.manage`.

## Access decisions fail closed

`com.atproto.managedspace.checkAccess` is the endpoint the Space's PDS
calls before issuing a credential for a managed space. It takes the `space`, the
account asking as `repo`, and the attested `clientId` when there is one, and
answers `{ read, write }`. It answers from the AppView's local projections and
makes no outbound call of its own.

The AppView checks these in order:

- A caller other than the space's authority gets `read: false, write: false`.
- A request without a `repo` gets the same.
- A space whose authority is not a Space on this AppView gets the same.
- The Space's own DID is allowed everything.
- A banned account is refused everything.
- An unrecognised space type is refused.

After that, the answer depends on the space type.

| Space | Read | Write |
| --- | --- | --- |
| profile | members | nobody but the Space |
| configuration, members | members | nobody but the Space |
| moderation | members holding `moderation.viewLog` | nobody but the Space |
| text channel | `channel.view` in the channel | `message.send` or `message.react` in the channel |
| voice channel | `voice.chat` in the channel | `voice.chat`, plus `message.send` or `message.react`, in the channel |
| thread | may read the thread | may post or react in the thread |

A write credential covers every collection in the space, so a member who may
only react still gets one. The AppView checks each record against its own
collection when it indexes the record: a `social.colibri.beta.reaction` record
needs `message.react`, and every other record needs the right to post. It skips
the records that fail.

People who haven't joined a Space get its name, description and images from
the AppView's index, for example in an invitation preview. The PDS refuses them
a credential for the profile space itself.
