---
title: Communities and AppViews
description: A community lives on one AppView, and what that means if you use another.
---

Colibri is self-hostable, so there is more than one AppView. A community lives
on exactly one of them: the instance that created it and holds its credentials.

A community's data sits in [permissioned spaces](/docs/architecture/spaces),
readable only by holding a key the community issues. Only the managing instance
holds one, so an AppView with no key for a community cannot read it and cannot
show it to you.

## What that means for you

**Use a community on the AppView that hosts it.** Reading, posting and
moderating all happen there. Your account is the same everywhere, so this is a
matter of which instance your client is pointed at, not of having several
identities.

**Presence stays inside one instance.** Online status, typing indicators and
voice presence come from the AppView you are signed in to, and reach nobody on
another one.

## Whether this changes

For one AppView to serve a community hosted by another, it would have to hold a
credential for that community's spaces, and credentials are issued per reader by
the community itself. A member's own client can mint the token that stands
behind such a credential, so there is a path to it. It is not built and it is
not scheduled.

## If something does not work

- **A community you are in does not appear.** It is hosted on a different
  AppView than the one you are signed in to. Point your client at that instance.
- **You cannot moderate, but you can read.** That is a role and permission
  question rather than an AppView one. Ask an admin of that community to check
  your roles.
- **Nothing loads and the error mentions credentials.** The AppView that manages
  that community may be unreachable. Its communities are unreadable while it is
  down, including to people who are not on it.
