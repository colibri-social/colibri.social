Blocking is a personal filter. After you block someone, they can't notify you, and their messages are collapsed wherever you meet them. The person isn't told.

A block changes only your own view. The person stays in the Spaces you share. Removing someone from a Space is up to its moderators.

## Block a person

<Steps>

1. Open the menu of the person in the member list.

2. Select **Block**, followed by their name.

3. Select **Block** to confirm.

</Steps>

## Effects of a block

- You get no notifications from the person, mentions and replies included.
- Their messages are collapsed and marked **Message from someone you blocked**. Select **Show** to read one, and **Hide again** to collapse it.
- A reply to one of their messages is marked **Replying to someone you blocked.**
- Their profile is covered with **You blocked this person**. Select **Show profile** to look at it anyway.

## Unblock a person

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and select **Blocked users**.

2. Select **Unblock** next to the person.

</Steps>

You can also open the person's menu and select **Unblock**, followed by their name.

## Someone is causing trouble in a Space

A block protects only you. If someone breaks the rules of a Space, tell its moderators. They can hide messages and remove the person for everyone.
