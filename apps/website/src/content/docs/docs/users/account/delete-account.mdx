You can delete everything Colibri holds about you yourself, at any time.

Deleting removes what you did in Colibri: your profile, your messages and your memberships. Your Atmosphere account stays as it is, along with the other apps you use it for.

## Delete your data

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and select **Advanced**.

2. Under **Danger zone**, select **Delete my Colibri data**.

3. Read what deleting removes, then type your handle under **Your handle**.

4. Select **Delete my Colibri data**.

</Steps>

Colibri shows its progress and confirms with **Your Colibri data is deleted**.

:::caution
Deleting your Colibri data can't be undone.
:::

If you have uninstalled the app, sign in at [colibri.social/app/settings/deleteAccount](/app/settings/deleteAccount) in your browser and follow the same steps from step 3.

## Deleted data

- Your Colibri profile and status
- Every message and reaction you've sent
- Your Space memberships
- Your notifications and what you've read
- Any invite links you made
- Notifications from Colibri on your devices

Your messages disappear for the other members of your Spaces. Bans and kicks recorded against you are kept.

## Spaces you own

If you're the only owner of a Space, Colibri shows **Hand over your Spaces first** and lists those Spaces. Give ownership to someone else or delete each Space, then come back.

To hand a Space over, open **Manage Space** and select **Transfer ownership**. To delete it, select **Delete Space** there. Owners need to [Transfer or Delete a Space](/docs/users/spaces/transfer-or-delete).

## Data that stays

Your Atmosphere account stays. Your handle and everything outside Colibri live with your account provider, which Colibri doesn't run.

The deletion page tells you where to delete that account too. It links to the account settings of your provider, or it shows the email address of the people who run it.

On Bluesky, delete your account under [Settings > Account](https://bsky.app/settings/account) in the Bluesky app.

## If something goes wrong

- **Deleting didn't finish**: Colibri shows what went wrong. Select **Delete my Colibri data** again.
- **A few items are still saved**: Colibri has removed your data, and a few items are still stored with your Atmosphere account. Log in again and delete once more to remove them.

If you're still stuck, or you can't sign in at all, email **pds@colibri.social**, send us your handle, and we'll help you figure it out!
