Your profile is your name tag in Colibri! Other people see it when they select your name or your picture: your display name, your handle, your pronouns, your bio and your roles in the Space you share.

You have one Colibri profile, and it looks the same in every Space.

## Open your profile editor

<Tabs syncKey="device">
  <TabItem label="Computer">
    Select your name at the bottom left, then select **Edit profile**. You can also open **Settings** and select **Profile**.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Phone">
    Select the **Profile** tab, then select **Edit profile**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Change your name, pronouns and bio

<Steps>

1. Open your profile editor.

2. Enter a **Display name**. This is the name shown on your messages. If you leave it empty, Colibri shows your handle.

3. Enter your **Pronouns** (for example `they/them`). They're shown next to your handle on your profile.

4. If you want, write a **Bio** of up to 256 characters.

5. Select **Save**.

</Steps>

## Change your picture and banner

<Steps>

1. Open your profile editor.

2. Select your picture to choose a new one, and crop it.

3. Select the banner area above it to add a banner.

4. Select **Save**.

</Steps>

To go back to no picture, select **Remove picture**.

## Pick your profile colors and badge

- **Theme colors** set the colors of your profile.
- **Badge** picks which badge is shown next to your name. It appears when you have more than one badge. **Automatic** shows your highest-ranked badge.

## Keep your profile in sync with Bluesky

If you also use Bluesky, Colibri can copy your profile from there. Turn on **Keep in sync with Bluesky** in the profile editor.

While it's on, your name, picture, banner and bio mirror your Bluesky profile, and you edit them in Bluesky. Your pronouns stay editable in Colibri. Leave them empty to show your Bluesky pronouns.

Turn the switch off to give your Colibri profile its own name, picture and bio.

## Change your handle, email or password

These belong to your Atmosphere account, which your account provider runs. Open **Settings** and select **My Atmosphere Account** to go to your provider's account page. The entry appears when your provider has such a page.
