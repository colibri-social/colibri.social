A bridge connects a Colibri Space to a group on another chat service (e.g. Discord). Picture an interpreter standing in a doorway between two rooms: no matter what is said in one room, the interpreter repeats it in the other!

A bridge carries messages, edits, deletions and reactions in both directions. It only works in the channels that an admin of the Space links to it.

A bridge is a separate program that someone runs next to Colibri. Anyone can run one!

:::note[Bridges are in beta]
Bridges are free to use while they are in beta. After that, they will cost $5 a month per Space.
:::

## Bridges for members

A message that arrives through a bridge shows the name and the picture its author has on the other service. It has no Colibri profile behind it.

You can mention people who post through a bridge. Type `@` in a channel, and anyone who recently posted there through the bridge appears under **Bridged**. The bridge turns your mention into a mention on the other service, so the person is notified there.

Reactions from the other service count once per person. Five people reacting with the same emoji over there show as five here.

Every member can see which bridges are connected to the Space.

## Bridges for admins

A member with the **Manage Space** permission can:

- pair a bridge with the Space or add it by its address, and disconnect it
- link and unlink channels, and pick the channels a bridge can read and reply in
- import a channel's earlier history
- turn moderation mirroring on and off
- pause the bridge

[Pairing a Bridge](/docs/users/bridges/pairing) has more information on each of these.

A bridge can only read and write in the channels it's linked to or allowed to reply in, and it can only edit, delete or unreact what it wrote itself. Disconnecting a bridge stops it immediately. Messages it already carried over stay in the channel.

## Carried-over content

| Carried over | Left out |
| --- | --- |
| Messages, with replies, file attachments and forwards | Private threads |
| Edits and deletions | Forum channels |
| Reactions with standard emoji | Role mentions |
| Threads beside linked channels | Custom emoji and stickers |
| Mentions of people and of linked channels | Link previews |
| Author names and pictures, in both directions | Typing indicators |
| Moderation, as described under [Moderation](#moderation) | Earlier Colibri history |
| Earlier history from the other service, as described under [History](#history) | |

A role mention on the other service arrives in Colibri as plain `@name` text.

## Threads

A thread opened beside a linked channel is carried over along with the channel, from either side. Threads that only some roles or members can read are left out.

Renaming a thread renames its copy. Deleting a thread in Colibri archives and locks its copy on the other service, which keeps that service's history.

## Moderation

When a moderator hides a message that came from Colibri, the bridge deletes its copy on the other service.

An admin can also turn on **Mirror moderation** for a bridge. Moderation then crosses in both directions:

- Hiding a message that came from the other service removes the original there.
- When a moderator on the other service removes the copy of a Colibri message, the message is hidden in Colibri.

Unhiding a message in Colibri doesn't restore anything on the other service.

## History

When an admin links a channel, they can bring in the earlier messages of the other side too: the last day, 7 days, 30 days or all of it.

- Imported messages keep the time they were sent, so they sit in the channel's history in order.
- They don't send notifications, and they don't mark the channel as unread.
- Public threads opened in that range come across with their messages.
- Reactions on imported messages aren't imported.
