Signing in involves Colibri and the provider of your Atmosphere account. When sign-in fails, Colibri shows a message that tells you which of the two is involved. Find your message below.

## Colibri can't find your handle

Colibri couldn't find an account with the handle you typed.

- Check the handle for typos. It usually looks like `name.bsky.social`.
- Enter the full handle, including the part after the first dot.

## Sign-in was cancelled

The page of your account provider was closed, or the permissions weren't approved. Nothing was shared with Colibri. Start again and approve the permission screen to finish.

## That sign-in took too long / That sign-in expired

A sign-in has to be finished within a few minutes. Start again from **Start setup**.

## Finish signing in with your account provider

Your provider wants you to sign in on its own page first. Sign in there, then come back to Colibri and try again.

## Your account provider isn't responding

The provider of your account can't be reached at the moment. Colibri doesn't run that provider. Try signing in again in a few minutes.

## Sign-in isn't working right now

The problem is on Colibri's side. We've been notified, try again in a little while.

## The browser didn't open

The Colibri apps hand sign-in to your browser and show **Finish signing in in your browser**. If no browser window appeared, select **Try again**. To start over with a different handle, select **Back**.

## Please sign in again

You were signed out. Sign in again. Your messages and your Spaces are still there.

## Colibri needs a quick approval

Colibri is missing a permission it needs. Sign in once more and approve the permission screen. Check our explainer about [the permission screen](/docs/users/getting-started/sign-in#the-permission-screen) for more info.

## Sign-up isn't available right now

Creating a new account through Colibri is paused. Signing in with an account you already have still works.

## A forgotten password

Your password belongs to your account provider, and Colibri can't reset it. Use the password reset of your provider. If your provider has an account page, **Settings** > **My Atmosphere Account** opens it once you're signed in.
