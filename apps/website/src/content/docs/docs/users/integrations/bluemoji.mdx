[Bluemoji](https://moji.blue) is a shared format for custom emoji in the Atmosphere. An app that supports it saves your emoji and your emoji packs to your Atmosphere account, where every other Bluemoji-compatible app can read them!

Colibri saves your own custom emoji as Bluemoji. This means:

- The emoji and packs you create in Colibri can be used by other apps that support Bluemoji.
- The emoji and packs you created in another Bluemoji app appear in **Settings** > **My emoji packs** in Colibri.

Following someone else's emoji works the same way for emoji from any app that supports Bluemoji!
