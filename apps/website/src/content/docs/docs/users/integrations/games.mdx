Colibri can show the game you're playing on your profile and next to your name in the member list! It reads this from [atmosphere.games](https://atmosphere.games), which saves your playing status to your Atmosphere account.

## Turn on sharing

Sharing is off by default.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and select **Chat**.

2. Under **Activity**, turn on **Share what I'm doing**.

</Steps>

The same switch also shares what you're listening to. Turning it off clears your activity for everyone right away.

## What others see

Your profile shows a **Playing** card with the name of the game, and its picture when the game has one. When atmosphere.games saves a link, selecting the card opens it. In the member list, the name of the game appears under your name.

The status disappears when atmosphere.games marks it as over, or after about ten minutes without an update.
