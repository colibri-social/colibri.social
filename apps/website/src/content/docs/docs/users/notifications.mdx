Colibri notifies you about three things: mentions, replies to your messages, and new messages in your Spaces. Two controls decide how much of that reaches you.

- A **notification level** is a volume knob. It sets how much you hear from all your Spaces, or from one Space.
- A **mute** is an off switch for one Space, category, channel or thread.

## Notification triggers

- **Mentions**: someone mentions you, or a role you hold.
- **Replies**: someone replies to one of your messages.
- **Messages**: any other new message in a channel you can read. In a thread, this covers the threads you follow and the threads you've posted in.

Colibri sends no notification for your own messages, for messages from people you blocked, or for a channel you have open at that moment.

## Get fewer notifications everywhere

Your notification level applies to every Space you've joined.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and select **Notifications**.

2. Under **Notify me about**, pick a level.

</Steps>

| Level | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **All messages** | Every new message in the Spaces you've joined. |
| **Mentions and replies** | Only when someone mentions you or replies to you. |
| **Nothing** | No notifications at all. |

A new account starts on **All messages**.

## Set a different level for one Space

Use this for a busy Space where you only care about mentions, or for a small Space where you want to see everything.

<Steps>

1. Open the menu of the Space and select **Notification settings**.

2. Under **Notify me about**, pick **All messages**, **Mentions and replies** or **Nothing**.

</Steps>

**Follow my default** returns the Space to the level from your settings.

## Mute a noisy channel

Open the menu of the channel and select **Mute channel**. A muted channel sends you no notifications, mentions included.

The same works for a category, a Space and a thread:

- **A category**: open the menu of the category and select **Mute category**. This mutes every channel in it.
- **A Space**: open the menu of the Space and select **Mute Space**. This also hides the unread markers and mention counts of the Space.
- **A thread**: open the menu of the thread and select **Mute thread**.

Select the same entry again to unmute.

:::tip
When you join a Space through an invite link, the invite screen has a **Mute this Space** switch. Turn it on to join the Space muted!
:::

## Follow a thread

You're notified about a thread once you've posted in it, if the thread has been made based on a message from you, or if you've been mentioned in one. To get notifications for a thread you only read, open the menu of the thread and turn on **Follow thread**. Turn it off to stop.

## Turn on notifications on your device

Each device has its own switch.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** and select **Notifications**.

2. Turn on **Notifications on this device**.

3. Allow notifications when your browser or your device asks.

</Steps>

If Colibri reports that it isn't allowed to show notifications, allow them for Colibri in your browser or system settings, then turn the switch on again.

**Sound effects volume** on the same page sets how loud message pings and call sounds are.

## Notification delivery

What happens while Colibri is closed depends on where you use it:

| Where | Notifications arrive |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone, iPad and Android apps, and the Mac app from the App Store | While Colibri is in the background or closed |
| Browser | While Colibri is in the background or its tab is closed, in browsers that support push notifications |
| Windows and Linux apps, and the Mac app downloaded from colibri.social | While Colibri is running |

Colibri holds back the notification on your devices in three cases:

- Your online status is **Do not disturb**.
- You're active in Colibri on one of your devices.
- The message is in the text chat of a voice channel.

## The Inbox

The **Inbox** collects your mentions and replies in one list, so nothing gets lost while you're away! Its **Mentions** and **Replies** tabs filter the list, and **Mark all as read** marks everything in it as read.

## The badge on the app icon

The badge on the Colibri icon counts your unread mentions and replies. Other new messages don't add to it, and mentions and replies in a muted Space are left out.
