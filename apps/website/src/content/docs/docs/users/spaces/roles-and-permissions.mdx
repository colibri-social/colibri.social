A permission is the right to do one thing in a Space, for example to send messages or to create channels. A role is a named set of permissions that you hand to members.

Think of a role as a key ring. Each permission is a key, and a member can carry several key rings. A member can open every door that any of their keys fits!

Roles live in **Manage Space** under **Roles**. To get there, open the menu of the Space and select **Manage Space**.

## The owner

The person who creates a Space is its owner. The owner can do everything in the Space, no matter what their roles say.

A crown next to the owner's name marks them as **Space Owner**. To hide it, turn off **Show owner crown** under **Roles**. This needs the **Manage Space** permission.

## The Members role

Every Space has a role named **Members**. Everyone who joins the Space gets it. You can change what it allows, but you can't delete it.

In a new Space, **Members** allows:

- **View channels**
- **Send messages**
- **Add reactions**
- **Create threads**
- **Join voice channels**, **Speak**, **Listen**, **Use camera**, **Share screen** and **See text chat**

:::caution
A permission you turn on for **Members** applies to everyone in the Space.
:::

## Create a role

You need the **Manage roles** permission.

<Steps>

1. In **Manage Space**, select **Roles**, then **Create role**.

2. Enter a **Role name** and pick a **Color**.

3. Turn on **Display separately** to list members with this role in their own group in the member list.

4. Turn on **Mentionable** to let other members mention the role with `@`.

5. Open **Permissions** and turn on what the role allows.

6. Open **Members** and add the people who get the role.

</Steps>

## Give a member a role

Open the menu of the member and select **Roles**, then turn roles on or off. You can also add people from the role itself, under **Members**.

## Role order

The order of the list under **Roles** is a ranking, with the highest role at the top.

- With **Manage roles**, you can edit and assign the roles below your own highest role.
- Roles at or above your highest role are locked for you.
- You can only turn on permissions that you hold yourself.

Kicking and banning follow the same ranking. A member can remove someone whose highest role is below their own.

## Permissions and their consequences

| Group | Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space | **Manage Space** | Edit the Space's name, description, icon, banner and join settings. |
| Space | **Delete Space** | Delete the Space and everything in it. |
| Approvals | **Manage approvals** | Approve or reject requests to join. |
| Categories | **Create categories**, **Edit categories**, **Delete categories** | Create, rename, reorder and delete categories. |
| Channels | **View channels** | See channels and read their messages. Without it, channels stay hidden. |
| Channels | **Create channels**, **Edit channels**, **Delete channels** | Create channels, rename them, change their settings and delete them. |
| Messages | **Send messages** | Write in text channels, voice channel chats and threads. Without it, they're read-only. In a voice channel's text chat, this also needs **See text chat**. |
| Messages | **Add reactions** | React to messages with emoji, even in read-only channels. In a voice channel's text chat, this also needs **See text chat**. |
| Members | **Kick members**, **Ban members**, **Unban members** | Remove members, ban them, and lift bans. |
| Roles | **Manage roles** | Create, edit and assign roles below your own highest role. |
| Moderation | **Apply labels** | Hide and unhide messages. |
| Moderation | **View moderation log** | See the Space's history of kicks, bans and unbans. |
| Invitations | **Create invitations**, **Delete invitations** | Create invite links and delete them. |
| Voice | **Join voice channels** | Connect to voice channels. |
| Voice | **Speak** | Talk in voice channels. Without it, members join muted and can't unmute themselves. |
| Voice | **Listen** | Hear others in voice channels. Without it, members join deafened and still see cameras and shared screens. |
| Voice | **Use camera**, **Share screen** | Turn on the camera and share the screen in voice channels. |
| Voice | **See text chat** | Read a voice channel's text chat. Without it, the chat is hidden. |
| Voice | **Moderate voice** | Mute, deafen, move and disconnect members in voice channels. |
| Mentions | **Mention all roles** | Mention roles that aren't marked as mentionable. |
| Threads | **Create threads** | Open threads beside a channel. |
| Threads | **Manage threads** | Rename, move and delete threads opened by anyone. |
| Threads | **Move messages** | Move other people's messages into another thread or channel. |

## Channel and category permissions

Roles apply to the whole Space. A channel or a category can make an exception for one role or one member. For example, an announcements channel can let only one role send messages, or a channel can hide itself from everyone except a few members.

<Steps>

1. Open the menu of the channel and select **Edit channel**. For a category, select **Edit category**.

2. Select **Channel permissions** or **Category permissions**.

3. Select **Add role or member** and pick who the exception is for.

4. Set each permission to **Allow**, **Deny** or **Inherit**.

   :::caution[TODO: screenshot]
   `channel-permissions.png`: Channel permissions with **Allow / Deny / Inherit** for one role
   :::

</Steps>

**Inherit** leaves the permission as the category and the roles already set it. An exception on a category applies to every channel in that category.

## Combining permissions

For each permission in a channel, Colibri checks these in order and uses the first one that says **Allow** or **Deny**:

1. The channel's setting for you as a single member.
2. The channel's settings for your roles, from your highest role down. **Members** comes last.
3. The category's setting for you as a single member.
4. The category's settings for your roles, in the same order.
5. The permissions of your roles.

Two rules of thumb follow from this. A setting for a single member wins over a setting for a role, and a channel wins over its category.

A member who can't view a channel can't do anything else in it. **View channels** is checked first, and the other permissions only count for members who pass it.

## Private channels and categories

When you create a channel, turn on **Make channel private?** to show it only to the members and roles you pick. **Make category private?** does the same for a category, and the channels inside follow it unless they have their own setting.
