Every Space has one owner. The owner can't leave the Space, so there are two ways to step away from it: hand it to someone else, or delete it.

Both actions are in **Manage Space**, under **Danger zone**.

## Transfer ownership

Transferring makes another member the owner. You give up everything that came with owning the Space. Only the current owner can start a transfer.

<Steps>

1. Open the menu of the Space and select **Manage Space**, then **Transfer ownership**.

2. Under **New owner**, search for the member and select them.

3. Tick **I understand that this action cannot be undone**.

4. Select **Transfer ownership**.

</Steps>

:::caution
You can't take the Space back yourself. Only the new owner can return it, by transferring ownership to you.
:::

The new owner has to be a member of the Space, and can't be banned from it.

## Delete a Space

Deleting removes the Space and all the data that belongs to it, for every member. You need the **Delete Space** permission, which the owner always has.

<Steps>

1. Open the menu of the Space and select **Manage Space**, then **Delete Space**.

2. Type the name of the Space to confirm.

3. Select **Delete Space**.

</Steps>

:::caution
A deleted Space can't be brought back.
:::

## Before you delete your Colibri data

If you're the only owner of a Space, Colibri asks you to hand it over or delete it before you can delete your own Colibri data. [Deleting Your Colibri Data](/docs/users/account/delete-account#spaces-you-own) has more information on that step.
