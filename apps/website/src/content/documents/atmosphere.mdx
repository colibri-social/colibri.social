## How Colibri differs from traditional chat platforms

A "normal" chat platform works like you'd expect. People send messages, images, and more to someone, and that someone stores the messages for them, then tells other people about them. That's how it's worked for the longest time. Discord, Slack and co. all work this way!

There's advantages to this. Instead of having to leave breadcrumbs on a website for people to find, and then respond with another breadcrump, you now have a way to communicate in real time.

But there are disadvantages, too. For example, when that platform ends up running out of money, or when it decides that certain features need to be locked behind a paywall, you can't simply move away. The messages you've sent, the conversations you've had, they're stuck with that platform. And migrating an entire community of tens, hundreds, or even thousands of people from one platform to another is a massive undertaking, one that will likely result in losing some people in the process. And that sucks.

Consider the alternative: what if your messages were stored in a way that they could be moved across apps? Instead of Discord or Slack storing your messages, you (or someone you trust) stores them instead, and the app you wish to use accesses the messages to display them to you, send new ones, and receive messages from others.

That's what Colibri is!

## How we're making sure your messages survive

Colibri is built on and believes in the concept of **decentralization**. We know that's a scary buzzword, but we think there's real benefits to knowing what it's about!

The core idea is that you don't depend on any one person or service for something. This can best be seen with, for example, email! You can send emails to people on GMail, Microsoft Outlook, or Apple Mail. It doesn't matter who manages their email address for them, they can still receive and send emails.

We're taking a similar approach by building on something called "AT Protocol": a technology that allows everyone to store data in a decentralized way.

Instead of relying on us to store your messages (which would be "centralized"), you have something called a "Personal Data Server" (PDS), which is responsible for storing your messages. Everyone who uses Colibri has one (meaning it's "decentralized", since there's not a single one)! All Colibri does is go around to every PDS, collects the messages for a channel, and then displays them to everyone else with access.

So how does that help? Well, the benefit is simple: on the off-chance that we ever happen to shut down, another app can come in, and still display your messages! You are no longer locked in to any app to talk to the people you want to talk to. You can even use different apps at the same time, based on what you like best.

## How we're making switching easier

We know that taking this first step is anything but easy. The people you hang out with probably have months of not years worth of messages stuck on "normal" apps like Discord.

That's why we have something called "Bridges", which make importing those old messages a breeze. We hope they make switching from existing platforms easier! Right now, we only have a bridge for Discord, but we expect this to rapidly improve soon. Let's just say we're working on things!
