Colibri is a small, non-commercial passion project. There's no big support
team behind it, just people who care about getting it right. If something's
broken, confusing, or you just have a question, here's how to reach us.

## Get in touch

For general questions, account help, or anything that doesn't fit below, email
us at **pds@colibri.social**. You can also reach us on the ATmosphere at
[@colibri.social on Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/colibri.social).

## Report a bug

Colibri is open source. The fastest way to get a bug fixed is to open an issue
on GitHub, where we can track it in the open:
[github.com/colibri-social/colibri.social/issues](https://github.com/colibri-social/colibri.social/issues).

If you'd rather not use GitHub, just email us at **pds@colibri.social** with what
happened and we'll take it from there.

## Request a feature

Have an idea for Colibri, or curious what we're already working on? Our public
feedback board lists everything that's planned or in development, and you can
suggest and vote on features there:
[Colibri Features on userinput.app](https://userinput.app/s/did:plc:mprdjqjluoswa7awzggaggj3/3msnhieoy7y2n).

## Report abuse or a safety concern

If something in a Space breaks that Space's own rules, tell its moderators
first. They can hide messages and remove people for everyone.

If you've come across illegal or harmful content, a bad-faith Space, or
anything that puts people at risk, including security issues, email
**pds@colibri.social**. Include:

- why you consider the content illegal or against the [Terms of Service](/tos)
- where it is: the Space, the channel and the message, or a screenshot that shows them
- your name and email address, unless the report is about child sexual abuse material
- a line confirming that you believe the report is accurate

We confirm that we got your report, look at the reported content, and tell you
what we decided.

## Disagree with a decision

If we restricted your content, your Space or your access, we tell you what we
did and why. If you disagree, or if we took no action on your report and you
think we should have, reply to our message or email **pds@colibri.social**
within six months and we'll look at it again.

## Response times

Because Colibri is run by volunteers in their spare time, we can't promise
instant replies. We read everything that comes in and do our best to get back
to you. Thanks for your patience.
