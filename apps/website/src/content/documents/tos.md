## 1. What Colibri is

Colibri is an open source app for text chat, voice and video calls in groups called **Spaces**. It is built on the
[AT Protocol](https://atproto.com).

- You sign in with an AT Protocol account from a provider of your choice. Colibri hosts no personal accounts.
- A Space is private. Its messages, reactions, threads and files can be read by the members who have access to the
  channel they are in.
- Your messages are stored in your own account where your provider supports private Space data. Where it does not,
  we store them for you until it does.
- The AppView keeps a copy of every Space it serves, delivers messages live, sends notifications, and carries calls.
- Space content is encrypted in transit. It is not end-to-end encrypted.

Your Colibri profile, custom emoji you upload, and badges are public records on the AT Protocol network.
