## 1. What Colibri is

Colibri is a free, open, real-time chat and community application built on the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com) (the
"Atmosphere"). Unlike a traditional platform, Colibri does not hold your data in a private silo:

- Your identity, messages, profile, and media live in a **Personal Data Server (PDS)**, either the Colibri-operated
  PDS (if you create a colibri.social account) or a third-party PDS (such as Bluesky or one you run yourself).

- **Communities** are their own repositories on the network.

A key consequence you accept by using the Service: **content you create on Colibri is public.** It is stored as public
records on the AT Protocol network, can be read and indexed by anyone (including other AppViews), and is not a private
message. Colibri does not currently offer end-to-end-encrypted private messaging.
